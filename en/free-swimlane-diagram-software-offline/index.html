Topogenia

Free swimlane diagram software that works offline

Topogenia draws your work processes as swimlane diagrams, one lane per actor. It runs on your Windows PC, without an account and without an internet connection.

A swimlane diagram tool that stays on your computer

Most swimlane diagram tools run in the cloud: you sign up, and your process maps live on someone else's servers. Topogenia takes the other road. You download one file, unzip it and launch it. The map opens in your browser on a local address, and nothing listens outside your workstation.

Your projects are kept in a single data file on your PC. There is no account to create, no subscription and no audience tracking.

Why offline matters

  • Confidential processes stay in-house. A process map shows who does what, with which documents: it does not have to leave the company.
  • It works on a closed network. No sign-in page to reach, no service to allow through the firewall.
  • Nothing to install. The portable version is a ZIP file: unzip and launch.
  • You keep the file. Back it up, move it, or share a project through a simple exchange package.

What you can map

  • Swimlane maps: one lane per actor, steps, decisions and parallel branches.
  • Nested maps: a step unfolds into its own diagram, as deep as needed.
  • A sheet per step: micro-tasks, exchanged documents, %C&A quality, pain points.
  • RACI, as a layer on the map or as a matrix.
  • Value stream: lead times, workload in FTE and main path, computed for you.
  • Exports to A3 PDF, PNG and Excel, and an interface in 18 languages.

An AI assistant, only if you want it

Topogenia can turn a plain-language story of your process into a swimlane diagram: you describe the work, the assistant proposes the map, and you preview it before applying it. This part is optional. It needs an internet connection and your own Anthropic or AWS Bedrock account, and it is called only when you ask. Everything else works offline.

Questions and answers

Is Topogenia really free?

Yes. The published releases are free to use, including for companies. Topogenia is published under the Apache-2.0 licence, except for its core, which is under a proprietary licence.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Drawing, editing, RACI, value stream and exports all work offline. Only the optional AI assistant needs a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no sign-in. Your data stays in a single file on your workstation.

Which systems does it run on?

Topogenia runs on 64-bit Windows. The interface opens in your usual browser.
